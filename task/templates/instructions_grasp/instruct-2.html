Instructions

The objects come in three shapes: a cube, a ball, and an oval-shaped object.

To give you a sense of their size, the cube’s side length, the ball’s diameter, and the oval-shaped object’s longest dimension are each approximately the length of a credit card, as illustrated below.


For each shape, the object’s size before deformation stays the same across trials, although it may deform differently when it lands. The animations are shown in slow motion.

Imagine that each object is lightweight and requires little effort to lift. Choose a grip that will keep it securely held throughout the move.
