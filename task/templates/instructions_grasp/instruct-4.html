Instructions

Once the message tells you to place your contact points, briefly left-click on the object’s surface wherever you want a contact point. Each successful click adds a blue dot, which marks one contact point.

You may place 1 to 5 contact points. The counter in the upper-left corner shows how many you have placed.

To change your selection, click “undo contact point” in the upper-left corner. Each click removes the most recently placed one. You can click it repeatedly to remove them all and start again.
