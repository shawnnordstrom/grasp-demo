Instructions

On each trial, you will watch a virtual object fall onto a flat, hard surface. All objects are released from rest at the same height above the surface. The object may bounce, wobble, or change shape as it lands. Watch it settle; some objects take longer than others.

A green rectangle on the surface marks the destination. Your goal is to lift the object, move it horizontally without dropping it, and set it down so that the entire object comes to rest within the rectangle.

Choose the placement that feels most natural to you to lift and move the object successfully.

Wait until the message tells you to place your contact points before clicking on the object.
