Main experiment

Next, you will begin the main experiment. Unlike the practice trial, you will not receive feedback. After choosing the number and locations of your contact points, click GO to submit your selection. You will not be shown whether your chosen grip would result in a successful move.

Remember, you can rotate the view and zoom in and out to inspect the object. To adjust your selection, click “undo contact point” in the upper-left corner to remove the most recently placed one, then click on the object to place it at a different location. You can click “undo contact point” repeatedly to remove more or start again.

Only click GO when you are satisfied with the number and locations of your contact points.

Click Next to begin.
