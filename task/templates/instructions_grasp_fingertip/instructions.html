Welcome to the experiment!

In this experiment, you will see virtual objects that have different shapes and are made of different materials. Your task is to choose where to place your fingertips on each object: the placement that feels most natural to you to lift and move it successfully to a target location.

The experiment takes about 15 to 20 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop computer and maximize your browser window before continuing. If you have any questions or problems, please contact cncl-participate@yale.edu.
