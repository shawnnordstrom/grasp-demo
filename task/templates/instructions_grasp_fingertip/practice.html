Congratulations on passing the quiz!

Before beginning the main experiment, you will practice on one object to become familiar with the controls.

During practice, try rotating the view and zooming in and out, placing fingers, and using “undo finger” to change your selection. Click GO to watch the attempt.

If your chosen number of fingers and their locations allow the object to be lifted and moved successfully to the green rectangle, you will see “You lifted it!” If the object slips out of your grip during the move, you will see “It slipped.” If it is carried but its centre comes to rest outside the green rectangle, you will see “It landed outside the green rectangle.”

After the attempt finishes, click try again to repeat the practice, or Next when you are ready to begin the main experiment. You may repeat the practice as many times as you like.
