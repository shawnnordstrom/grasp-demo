Instructions

Once the message tells you to place your fingers, briefly left-click on the object’s surface wherever you want a virtual fingertip to make contact. Each successful click adds a blue dot, which marks one fingertip’s contact location. You will see these dots rather than a complete virtual hand.

You may place 1 to 5 fingers. The counter in the upper-left corner shows how many you have placed.

To change your selection, click “undo finger” in the upper-left corner. Each click removes the most recently placed finger. You can click it repeatedly to remove all your fingers and start again.
