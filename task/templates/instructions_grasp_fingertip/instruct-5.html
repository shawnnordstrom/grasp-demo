Instructions

When you are satisfied with your selection, click GO in the upper-right corner. You can change the number and locations of your fingers before clicking GO, but each object in the main experiment allows only one attempt, so check your selection first.

The virtual fingers will automatically attempt to squeeze the object, lift it, carry it to the green rectangle, and set it down. The computer controls the squeezing force and movement; you only choose the number and locations of the fingers.

Watch the entire attempt. The object may deform or slip out of the grip, so successful placement is not guaranteed. Once the movement has finished and Next becomes available, click it to continue.
