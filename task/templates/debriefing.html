Debriefing

Thank you for your participation in our study! Your anonymous data makes an important contribution to our understanding of human learning and memory.

In this study we are investigating how people infer the material properties of soft objects from watching them move—and whether those inferences support good predictions about how to physically handle them. When you watched the object fall and settle, its wobble carried information about how soft and how heavy it is. Your choice of where to grip it tells us whether people use that information when planning a physical action.

The settling you watched was computed in advance and was identical for every participant. The pick-up that followed was simulated live, in response to the finger positions you chose—so what you saw was the actual physical consequence of your own choice, not a canned outcome.

If you have any questions about this research, you may contact the principal investigator, Principal Investigator.
